Syntax: <required>　[optional]

📊 Stats

wws <server> <nickname>Account overview (stats)
wws ship <ship name>Single-ship stats
wws recent [days or date]Recent battles for your account
wws recent_random [days or date]Recent random battles only (also: wws recent random)
wws recent_rank [days or date]Recent ranked battles only (also: wws recent rank)
wws ship <ship name> recent [days or date]Recent battles for a ship
wws recentsRecent battles (single battle view)
wws ships <tier/nation/type> [min N] [max N]Filter your ship list (e.g. ships bb 10 japan min 6 max 10)

🏆 Rankings

wws ship.rank <server> <ship name>Ship ranking
wws cw.rank [server] [season]Clan war ranking (empty = latest season, all servers)
wws clan.rank <server> <clan tag>Clan ranking

🛡️ Clan

wws clan <server> <clan tag>Clan overview
wws cw.recent <server> <clan tag> [season] [team]Clan war recent battles (me/@ also works)

🔗 Bind

wws bind <server> <nickname>Bind your game account
wws special_bind <AID>Bind by account ID (copy from website)
wws bind_list meList bound accounts
wws change_bind [index]Switch bound account (empty shows a picker)
wws delete_bind <index>Remove a bound account
wws update / wws me update / wws <server> <nickname> updateRefresh user data cache

wws boxChristmas box ship pool (me/@ or server+nickname)
wws search_ship <nation> <type> <tier>Search ship names by filters
